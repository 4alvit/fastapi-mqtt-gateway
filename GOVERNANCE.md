# Governance and maintenance

fastapi-mqtt-gateway is an authenticated REST/WebSocket bridge to MQTT, maintained publicly by [4alvit](https://github.com/4alvit). The owner is the primary maintainer, repository administrator and release decision maker. These are responsibilities, not a claim of independent people filling each role.

## Decisions and responsibilities

Contributors propose focused changes with tests and documentation. Reviewers check behavior, compatibility and security boundaries. The maintainer decides scope and merges changes after required checks and reviews; automated review assists but does not constitute a human successor. Release ownership and acceptance follow [RELEASING.md](RELEASING.md). The administrator maintains access controls and revokes compromised credentials.

Use public issues for design proposals, including the problem, alternatives and compatibility impact. Record the decision and its rationale in the issue or pull request. Contributors may request reconsideration with new evidence. Confidential vulnerabilities use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Continuity remains open

On 2026-10-13 the owner confirmed that no backup maintainer or verified access-recovery arrangement can take over repository administration and publication if the owner becomes unavailable. OpenSSF Silver `access_continuity` is therefore **unmet**. A public clone, CI bot or policy document does not close this gap.

Before changing that assessment, establish a consenting responsible person or actual recovery mechanism, inventory GitHub, CI, release and security-reporting access, and exercise recovery within one week without publishing secrets. Record the arrangement and review date here. No successor is assigned by this document.
